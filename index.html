Built for crew.
Not for everyone.

These tools exist because the official sources — OSS, IDTGR, carrier websites — don't give you the full picture fast. The ZED Atlas puts all 218 agreements side by side so you can compare, filter, and find the best routing in seconds, not minutes.

The FR24 data gives you ground truth on what carriers actually fly — not what they publish in schedules — so you know which agreements have real route depth behind them.

DISCLAIMER — For crew reference only.
Always verify ZED fares, eligibility, and availability in OSS / IDTGR before purchasing tickets. FR24 data is a sample (~20 flights/carrier/day) and may not reflect complete schedules. Fare data as of 30 Apr 2026.
