Built for crew.
Not for everyone.

These tools exist because the official sources — OSS, IDTGR, carrier websites — don't give you the full picture fast. The ZED Atlas puts all 218 agreements side by side so you can compare, filter, and find the best routing in seconds, not minutes.

The FR24 data gives you ground truth on what carriers actually fly — not what they publish in schedules — so you know which agreements have real route depth behind them.

DISCLAIMER — For crew reference only.
Always verify ZED fares, eligibility, and availability in OSS / IDTGR before purchasing tickets. FR24 data is a sample (~20 flights/carrier/day) and may not reflect complete schedules. Fare data as of 30 Apr 2026.

Data sources.

ZED & LT agreement data sourced from official Etihad crew portals and verified against carrier publications as of 30 April 2026. FR24 network data sampled May–Jun 2026 across 194 priority carriers.

Etihad financial and operational statistics from the FY 2025 Annual Report (published February 2026). New route information from official Etihad press releases and verified by Aviation A2Z, The Expat Flyer, and Engine Cowl analysis.

Airport reference database covers 1103 airports with ICAO/IATA codes, coordinates, and country data. World map geometry from Natural Earth public domain data.

IATA Network Strategy matrix adapted from industry publications — carrier positions estimated from published capacity data and new-route seat deployment ratios.
